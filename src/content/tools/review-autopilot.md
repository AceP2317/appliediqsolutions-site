### This one is not for sale, and the reason is the most useful thing on the page

Every other tool in this gallery is either something you can buy or proof of what gets built. This
is neither. It is kept open because what happened to it is worth showing.

It was the lead offer. In March 2026 a platform shipped the whole category built in and free, inside
software the buyer already opens every day. Overnight there was no price, no feature and no trust
position left in it.

It is still here, still working, and nobody is being asked to pay for it. Selling it at the price of
a fitted tool would be selling the exact thing that taught this practice what not to build.

### What it taught, and what the shelf does differently

A tool is safe to build when the rule inside it belongs to the **owner** rather than to the trade.

Answering reviews is identical work in every business on the high street, so any platform can absorb
it and eventually one did. Splitting a night's tips by a rule one bar invented, or counting a back
bar the way one owner counts it, is not identical anywhere — and a platform cannot ship it, because
there is nothing general to ship.

That distinction is why the shelf exists and what everything on it is chosen against. This page is
where it was learned.

### What the tool does

Reviews from Google, Yelp and Facebook land in one queue instead of three tabs. Each one gets a
reply drafted in the business's own voice, ready to edit or post.

Switch between the five sample businesses — an auto shop, a restaurant, a salon, a dental practice
and an HVAC company — and the whole console re-skins, reviews and all. An owner sees their own kind
of business rather than somebody else's.

### The autopilot rule, which never bent

**A positive review posts automatically. A neutral one waits as a draft. A negative review is always
held for the owner.**

No critical reply has ever left this tool without a person deciding. That was not a setting and it
was never configurable, because the one thing worse than an unanswered complaint is an automated
answer to one — public, permanent, and obviously written by nobody.

That rule survives its product. It is the shape every automation on the shelf is still built to.

### What it will not do

**The replies here are pre-written samples.** Nothing is generated live on this page. The drafting
path was deliberately removed when the tool came off the shelf.

**Nothing about the drafting is trained.** There is no model behind it and there never was one in
this demo. Saying otherwise would be the sort of claim the rest of this site exists to refuse.

**It posts nothing anywhere.** No account is connected, no review platform is contacted, and nothing
you click here leaves the page.

**The businesses are invented.** Marker Nine is not a real town and none of these five are real
companies. The reviews are written, not collected.

**It is not on the price list and will not be quoted.** If review responses matter to your business,
the honest answer is that the platform you already pay for probably does it now — and that answer is
free.
