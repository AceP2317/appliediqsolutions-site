### The commission is not the interesting number

Every host on the platform pays the same rate. It is on your payout statement, it is the same figure
this month as last, and a tool built around it would be doing arithmetic the statement already did
for you.

Two figures are yours and neither appears on any statement you receive: **what a turn costs your
house**, and **how the occupancy tax was handled**.

### The tax mistake, which is the common one

**Tax the platform collects on top and remits** is not your income. It never touched you. It should
not appear in your gross and it should not appear in what you kept.

**Tax you collected** is money you owe. It is in your account and it is not yours.

Counting the first as revenue is the single most frequent way this sum goes wrong, and it goes wrong
in a pleasant direction — your gross looks bigger and your margin looks better, on every booking, all
year. Then the picture of the business you have been carrying around is not the business.

Both are handled explicitly here, as a choice you make rather than an assumption made for you.

### The cleaning fee against the actual turn

This is the finding.

A cleaning fee is set once, in a listing, probably by looking at what other listings charge. A turn
cost is worked out never. So the two numbers have no relationship to each other at all, and the gap
between them is invisible in both directions.

**Charge over your turn cost** and the surplus is quietly subsidizing your nightly rate. Your rate
looks more competitive than it is, and you do not know by how much.

**Charge under it** and the house absorbs part of every single turn, on every booking, forever, with
no line item anywhere that says so.

Both directions are printed beside the net. Most hosts have never seen either.

### A night at a time

The net is shown per night as well as per booking, because that is the number that compares.

A four-night booking and a two-night booking at the same rate do not leave the same money behind,
and the reason is the turn — which is the arithmetic the room turn cost sheet works through in
detail. The two tools are the same picture from either end.

### What it replaces

The payout statement, which tells you what arrived and nothing about what it cost. Or the spreadsheet
that nets commission off the gross and stops there, which is where most hosts stop.

### Who it is for

B&Bs, inns, short-term rentals and holiday cottages — anywhere bookings come through a platform and
the statement shows you revenue rather than what you kept.

### What it will not do

**It suggests no nightly rate and no cleaning fee.** It shows you what yours are doing.

**It does not know your tax obligations.** It handles the tax the way you tell it to. Which taxes
apply, who remits them and what you owe is between you and the authority where you are — this is
arithmetic, not advice.

**It does not price against other listings.** Nothing here comes from anybody's market data, and
nothing here knows what the cottage down the road charges.

**Nothing you type is sent anywhere.** The arithmetic runs in your browser. Booking revenue and cost
structure stay on the machine you typed them on. Close the tab and it is gone.
