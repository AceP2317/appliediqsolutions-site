summary: >-
  French AI lab Mistral AI raised €3 billion (about $3.58 billion) at a
  post-money valuation over €21 billion, in a Series D round led by Samsung
  Electronics with EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund and existing investor PSG
  Equity co-leading. Mistral calls it the largest equity fundraising round
  ever completed by a European technology company, and says the money will
  fund compute capacity, new data centers, and international expansion as
  it positions itself as a sovereign-AI alternative to U.S. labs. New
  backers include Advent, BlackRock, and Luxembourg; existing investors
  Nvidia, ASML, and Salesforce Ventures also joined.
implications: >-
  Sovereign AI infrastructure is becoming a real investment category, not
  just a talking point — a European lab now has real capital to compete on
  compute with the American labs it positions itself against, while
  promising customers more control over where their data and AI queries are
  processed. For a business weighing AI vendors, that's a genuine
  alternative with different data-residency guarantees, not just a smaller
  also-ran.
