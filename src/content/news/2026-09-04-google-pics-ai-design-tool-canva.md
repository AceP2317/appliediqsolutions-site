summary: >-
  Google began rolling out Google Pics on September 1, an AI image-creation
  and editing tool built into Google Workspace and powered by its Nano Banana
  image model. Rather than a design canvas, Pics works from text prompts to
  generate posters, social posts, and other everyday visuals, and includes
  editing tools to isolate objects, change or translate text inside images,
  and produce multiple versions to choose from. It starts inside Docs and
  Slides today and will later reach Google Drive, available to most Workspace
  customers and Google AI Pro or Ultra subscribers.
implications: >-
  For a small business already paying for Workspace, this folds a basic
  design tool into a subscription it already has, which is real downward
  pressure on standalone tools like Canva for simple marketing graphics. It's
  not a replacement for a designed brand identity or anything requiring a
  human's judgment on layout, but for a flyer or a social post it lowers the
  cost of "good enough" to whatever Workspace already costs.
