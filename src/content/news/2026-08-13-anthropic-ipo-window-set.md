summary: >-
  Anthropic has set its target IPO window for as early as September or
  October 2026, aiming to list on Nasdaq with Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs,
  and JPMorgan running the offering, according to a Wall Street Journal
  report from August 10 that Anthropic executives are meeting investors to
  build confidence ahead of the listing. The company's post-money valuation
  sits around $965 billion after its latest funding round, and a successful
  listing would make it the most valuable public AI company and put it
  ahead of rival OpenAI, which has reportedly pushed its own IPO into 2027.
implications: >-
  Investors pressing Anthropic in pre-IPO meetings are focused on real
  operating risks — high compute costs, cheaper Chinese open-weight models
  pulling customers toward lower-cost inference, and regulatory uncertainty
  — the same pressures any business betting on a single AI vendor's staying
  power should be weighing. A first-mover IPO would also make a frontier
  lab's financials public for the first time, showing what running one
  actually costs versus what it earns.
