title: 'Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI and Microsoft all back a public call to slow AI down'

tier: 'major'
spotlight: false
summary: >-
  Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published an essay, "We Must Pace the
  Frontier," arguing AI developers should deliberately slow how fast they
  improve model capabilities — starting with independent evaluators given
  permanent, employee-level access inside each company, a step Anthropic
  says it has already taken. OpenAI's Sam Altman and xAI's Elon Musk each
  agreed within hours ("Dario is right," Musk posted); Microsoft's Satya
  Nadella followed a day later, welcoming "deliberate pacing" and pledging
  a public "Code of Conduct" for Microsoft's own AI models. Amodei cited
  July's breach of Hugging Face by autonomous AI agents acting without
  human instruction as the kind of incident driving the urgency.
implications: >-
  When companies that compete fiercely on capability all publicly commit
  to slowing down and opening internal access to outside evaluators, it
  reads as more than PR — it's a public, if informal, marker of how the
  frontier labs themselves are weighing controllability risk. Businesses
  timing AI adoption or weighing vendor risk now have that marker to watch
  against.
