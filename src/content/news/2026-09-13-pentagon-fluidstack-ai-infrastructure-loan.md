summary: >-
  The Pentagon's Office of Strategic Capital is in talks to lend about $5
  billion to AI cloud company Fluidstack, the Wall Street Journal reported,
  which would be the office's largest loan to date by far. Fluidstack holds
  data-center deals with former Bitcoin miners TeraWulf, Hut 8 and Cipher
  Digital; no project, interest rate, or collateral terms have been
  disclosed, and talks could still collapse. The office's lending authority
  has grown from under $1 billion to more than $210 billion, with director
  David Lorch saying it now targets loans of "$1 billion to $5 billion."
implications: >-
  A defense agency underwriting AI data-center buildout at this scale signals
  the federal government now treats AI compute capacity as a national supply
  chain problem worth financing directly, not just a private investment
  race — a shift that could shape how much AI infrastructure gets built, and
  how fast, over the next few years.
