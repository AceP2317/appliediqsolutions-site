summary: >-
  OpenAI is developing a new model family, tentatively named Astra, built
  to coordinate multiple agents on long-running tasks; CEO Sam Altman has
  already demoed it to officials in Washington, D.C., per The Information.
  Astra is reportedly in testing and expected to be first through the
  Trump administration's planned framework requiring federal review before
  release; no release date or final name has been set. Separately, OpenAI
  said an internal Astra model solved ten previously unsolved math and
  theoretical computer science problems, at a token cost of about $2,000,
  though it did not crack any Millennium Prize Problems.
implications: >-
  Two things are moving in parallel here worth tracking separately: frontier
  labs' research capability climbing fast enough to produce genuinely new,
  human-checked mathematical results cheaply, and a first concrete case of
  a government pre-release review requirement for a frontier model. If that
  review framework becomes standard, it could shape how quickly new AI
  capabilities reach the market going forward.
