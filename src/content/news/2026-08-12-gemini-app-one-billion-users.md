summary: >-
  Google CEO Sundar Pichai announced the Gemini app has passed 1 billion
  monthly active users, the 14th Google product to hit that mark and the
  company's fastest-growing ever — up from 950 million at its Q2 earnings
  call weeks earlier. Google says 63% of Gemini users talk to it by voice,
  it generates more than 150 million images a day, and it now has over 100
  million active users on iOS alone. The milestone puts Gemini roughly on
  pace with ChatGPT, which passed 1 billion monthly users in June.
implications: >-
  Two general-purpose AI assistants have now each reached roughly a billion
  monthly users within months of each other, which means a large and
  growing share of any business's customers already have a default AI habit
  before they ever reach out to a company directly. That raises the bar for
  what a business's own AI-assisted contact points — a chatbot, a phone
  line, a booking flow — need to feel like by comparison, and makes "we
  don't have one yet" a more visible gap than it was a year ago.
