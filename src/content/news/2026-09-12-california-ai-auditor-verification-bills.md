summary: >-
  Governor Newsom signed Senate Bill 813 and Assembly Bill 1405 on September
  9, building on California's 2025 frontier-AI transparency law. SB 813
  creates a first-in-the-nation framework for independent organizations that
  can verify whether an AI system or model complies with state law; AB 1405
  creates a state registry for AI auditors and sets standards for their
  independence, transparency and integrity. Newsom's office said the bills
  respond to "increasingly urgent concerns about the risks posed by these
  systems" and called on the federal government to pass its own AI
  regulations to match.
implications: >-
  Once independent auditors have a legal standard to work from, a compliance
  claim about an AI system stops being something a vendor states about its
  own product and becomes something a third party can check. For any
  business buying or embedding AI, that shifts the question from "what does
  the vendor say" toward "can this be independently verified" — and a
  California-registered audit is likely to become a reference point other
  states and buyers cite too.
