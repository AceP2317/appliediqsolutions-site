summary: >-
  Sen. Jacky Rosen (D-NV), a Senate Commerce Committee member with a
  computer programming background, is calling on committee chairman Sen. Ted Cruz (R-TX)
  to promptly hold a hearing with executives from leading AI companies on the
  pace of AI development and what guardrails are needed. She cited a former
  Anthropic and OpenAI researcher's warning about humans' diminishing ability
  to control AI models, plus the recent joint call from Anthropic's Dario
  Amodei, OpenAI's Sam Altman and xAI's Elon Musk for more industry
  guardrails.
implications: >-
  A hearing would put AI company executives under direct, public questioning
  from lawmakers about safety practices, and could be an early marker of
  federal appetite for binding AI rules. Businesses currently navigate AI
  safety through a patchwork of voluntary vendor commitments and state law;
  a hearing doesn't change that today, but it's a signal worth watching.
