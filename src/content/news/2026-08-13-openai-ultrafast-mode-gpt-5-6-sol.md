summary: >-
  OpenAI introduced Ultrafast, a new preview mode for its GPT-5.6 Sol model
  that it says runs at 14 times the speed of standard processing, delivering
  up to 750 output tokens per second. The company said in a blog post that
  "until now, getting real-time speed typically meant choosing a smaller or
  more specialized model," and that Ultrafast is powered by its partnership
  with chipmaker Cerebras. OpenAI is currently offering the preview to a
  small group of API customers, with plans to expand access as capacity
  grows, and points to incident response, customer service and support,
  financial market analysis, and e-commerce as target use cases.
implications: >-
  Response latency is the difference between a tool that feels like a real
  conversation and one that feels like waiting on hold — a caller or chat
  customer notices a multi-second delay. Faster inference at similar model
  quality lowers the technical bar for building AI tools that need to feel
  instant, like a phone assistant or live chat, though for now the fastest
  version is limited to a small preview group.
