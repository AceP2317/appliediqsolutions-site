summary: >-
  Salesforce introduced seven named "job-ready" Agentforce agents —
  including a customer-service agent, an IT/HR agent, a supply-chain
  agent, and an outbound sales agent — each pre-built with the skills and
  data models for one business function and connected to a company's own
  Customer 360 data. Most are generally available now; the outbound sales
  agent is in pilot, using a new "long-horizon runtime" meant to pursue a
  goal over weeks instead of a single session. Salesforce said Agentforce
  and Slack combined have logged 7 billion "agentic work units" so far,
  3.2 billion of them in the most recent quarter alone.
implications: >-
  The shift from one general chat assistant to agents scoped to a single
  job is a bet that businesses adopt AI faster when it's pre-packaged
  around a specific task rather than left open-ended — a packaging
  question any business evaluating AI tools runs into regardless of
  vendor.
angle: >-
  It's the enterprise-scale version of the same bet the shelf makes at a
  much smaller scale: a tool built narrow around one job does more for a
  business than a general assistant asked to do everything.
