summary: >-
  Nvidia is discussing joining a new equity round that would value AI
  search startup Perplexity above $30 billion, up from $20 billion a year
  ago, The Information reported Sunday, citing people familiar with the
  talks; neither company confirmed it. Perplexity's annualized revenue has
  climbed above $750 million, up from under $250 million at the start of
  2026, growth partly driven by Perplexity Computer, its cloud-based AI
  agent for automating computer tasks. Nvidia is already an investor
  alongside Jeff Bezos and SoftBank; Perplexity has raised more than $1.5
  billion to date and has said it plans to go public in 2028.
implications: >-
  A roughly 40x-revenue valuation on real, fast-tripling revenue is a
  different signal than the valuation jumps driven mostly by hype elsewhere
  in AI funding — worth watching as an early read on what investors think
  AI-agent products that automate real work are worth once they're
  generating cash, not just usage.
