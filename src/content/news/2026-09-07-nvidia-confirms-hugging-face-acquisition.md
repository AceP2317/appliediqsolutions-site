summary: >-
  Nvidia confirmed on September 3 that it has agreed to acquire Hugging
  Face, the open-source AI hub used by more than 18 million developers,
  for $12,930,300,000 — ending weeks of reported talks. CEO Jensen Huang
  wrote that Hugging Face will remain an open platform where developers
  keep choosing their own models, frameworks, clouds and compute, and
  that "Nvidia compute will not be required to build on or deploy through
  Hugging Face." Hugging Face CEO Clem Delangue said the company sought
  Nvidia's backing for the compute and support needed to keep scaling.
implications: >-
  A chipmaker now owns one of the central distribution points for
  open-source AI models and datasets — leverage that matters even to
  developers with no direct Nvidia hardware relationship. Nvidia's public
  commitment to keep the platform open is the promise worth watching: it
  costs nothing to make today and everything if it's quietly walked back
  once the deal closes.
