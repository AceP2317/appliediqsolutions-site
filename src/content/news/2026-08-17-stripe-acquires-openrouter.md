summary: >-
  Stripe has finalized a deal to acquire OpenRouter for more than $7 billion,
  Bloomberg reported August 16, roughly five times the $1.3 billion valuation
  OpenRouter carried after a $113 million Series B just three months earlier.
  OpenRouter gives developers a single API to route across more than 400 AI
  models from providers including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google, and says it
  serves 8 million users. A Stripe spokesperson told TechCrunch the company
  does not comment on rumors or speculation, leaving the deal formally
  unconfirmed even as multiple outlets converge on the same figure.
implications: >-
  A payments company buying the market's leading AI-model gateway signals
  that routing between AI providers — picking the cheapest or best model for
  a given task on the fly — is becoming infrastructure worth owning outright,
  not just a startup niche. Any business whose product depends on an
  independent, neutral routing layer should watch how Stripe integrates or
  prices OpenRouter post-acquisition; the "avoid vendor lock-in" pitch gets
  harder to make once the router itself belongs to one company.
