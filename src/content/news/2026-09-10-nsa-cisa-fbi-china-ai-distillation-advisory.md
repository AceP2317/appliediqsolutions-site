summary: >-
  The NSA, CISA, and FBI issued a joint cybersecurity advisory on September 8
  accusing six Chinese AI companies — DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Alibaba,
  MiniMax, StepFun, and Z.AI — of systematically extracting capabilities from
  leading U.S. AI models since late 2024, likely with the Chinese
  government's knowledge. The advisory says DeepSeek drew on Claude, GPT, and
  Gemini to train its own models, and that Moonshot AI extracted output from
  Claude Fable and GPT-4o, using fraudulent accounts and proxy routing to
  avoid detection. Rather than recommend blocking suspect accounts outright,
  the agencies advised U.S. AI companies to quietly degrade their responses
  instead.
implications: >-
  For any business relying on a U.S. AI vendor's usage terms or output
  quality as a competitive edge, this is a reminder that the edge can erode
  indirectly — through data used elsewhere to train a rival model, not
  through anything the business itself did. It also raises the security
  stakes of which country's AI tools a business routes its own data through.
