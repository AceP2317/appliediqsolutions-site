summary: >-
  Microsoft AI released MAI-Transcribe-2 on September 4, pricing it at a
  limited-time 10 cents per audio hour — about 72% below its prior model's
  rate and cheaper than OpenAI's, Google's and ElevenLabs' transcription
  services. Microsoft says independent tests show it running roughly 10
  times faster than OpenAI's GPT-Transcribe, with a 5.2% average error rate
  across 60 languages on the public FLEURS benchmark, plus built-in speaker
  labeling and audit-ready formatting. The promotional price runs through
  the end of 2026, and Microsoft hasn't said what it will cost after that.
implications: >-
  For any business paying to transcribe calls, meetings or field recordings,
  this is a real near-term price drop worth testing. But Microsoft itself
  classifies the model as a public preview with no service-level agreement
  and doesn't recommend it for production workloads yet, and the discount
  price is temporary — worth piloting on real recordings now, not migrating
  a mission-critical workflow onto it today.
angle: >-
  Vendor transcription pricing is a direct input cost for any tool built to
  summarize calls or job walk-throughs — a genuine drop like this is worth
  factoring into what a build costs before quoting one.
