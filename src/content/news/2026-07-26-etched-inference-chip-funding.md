summary: >-
  Etched, a three-year-old AI hardware startup, closed a $300 million Series
  C on July 23 at a $10.3 billion valuation, led by Sequoia — double its $5
  billion valuation from a round just seven months earlier. The company
  builds the Sohu chip, which hardcodes the transformer architecture used by
  most modern AI models directly into silicon rather than relying on
  general-purpose GPUs, and says it has booked over $1 billion in signed
  customer contracts with first systems shipping this summer. Co-founder
  Robert Wachen says the systems can run any transformer-based model, not
  just one specific model as early skeptics assumed.
implications: >-
  It's a sign that investors are still betting heavily on specialized
  inference hardware as a way to cut the cost of running AI models at scale,
  rather than treating Nvidia's general-purpose GPUs as the only viable
  option. For any business paying per-token for AI features, competition at
  the inference-chip layer — not just at the model layer — is one of the
  more likely sources of falling AI costs over the next year or two.
