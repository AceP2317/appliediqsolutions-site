summary: >-
  Sakana AI released Fugu Max and Fugu Ultra v2, two versions of its Fugu
  "orchestrator" model that routes each task to the cheapest model in a pool
  capable of solving it rather than being a single foundation model itself.
  Fugu Max optimizes for cost — priced at $2/$6 per million tokens, which
  Sakana says runs 40-60% cheaper than comparable models on output while
  topping six benchmarks — while Fugu Ultra v2 targets harder, multi-step
  reasoning and coding work. Both ship today as a hosted API only, with no
  open weights and no availability in the EU or EEA.
implications: >-
  Splitting one architecture into a cheap-first and capable-first version is
  a bet that most real AI workloads don't need the most expensive model
  available — a useful data point for any business trying to reason about
  what it's actually paying for when it buys access to a general AI system.
