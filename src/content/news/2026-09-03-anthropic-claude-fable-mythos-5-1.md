summary: >-
  Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1 on September 1 —
  the same underlying model at two safeguard levels, with Mythos 5.1 limited
  to trusted-access programs for cybersecurity and life-sciences work. Fable
  5.1 costs an estimated 25% less than its predecessor for typical workloads,
  and up to roughly 45% less for heavily agentic tasks, from cheaper
  cache-read pricing. Anthropic also says its newest cybersecurity
  safeguards now flag roughly 60% fewer false positives than before, and is
  rolling out "Enterprise Frontier Safeguards," which stores
  customer data in infrastructure the customer controls rather than
  Anthropic's, starting this fall.
implications: >-
  For anyone running AI-assisted workflows, the direction is falling
  per-task cost on agentic (multi-step, tool-using) work specifically —
  the category most likely to show up in day-to-day business automation —
  plus a real move toward customer-controlled data storage rather than
  just a policy promise. Worth revisiting vendor pricing and data-handling
  terms now rather than assuming last quarter's numbers still hold.
angle: >-
  AppliedIQ's own drafting tools run on this model family, so a genuine
  cost drop on agentic work is one that gets passed through rather than
  absorbed — it's a direct input cost, not a marketing line.
