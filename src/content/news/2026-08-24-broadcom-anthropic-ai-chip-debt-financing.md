summary: >-
  Broadcom is in talks with lenders to raise more than $60 billion in debt
  for an AI chip financing arrangement that could grow to around $100
  billion once junior financing is included, according to reporting
  relayed August 21. The money would back AI infrastructure involving
  Anthropic and potentially other major AI companies, building on
  Broadcom's earlier work with Blackstone and Apollo on financing tied to
  Anthropic's compute buildout. Broadcom has become central to
  hyperscalers seeking custom accelerators that reduce dependence on
  Nvidia GPUs.
implications: >-
  Rather than AI infrastructure sitting on tech companies' own balance
  sheets, chipmakers, private credit firms, banks, and institutional
  investors are increasingly building special-purpose debt structures
  around expected future compute demand — turning AI capital spending into
  its own asset class. For any business evaluating an AI vendor's
  staying power, financing structure this large and this leveraged is
  worth watching alongside the vendor's actual revenue.
