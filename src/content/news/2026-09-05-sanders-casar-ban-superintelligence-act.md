summary: >-
  Sen. Bernie Sanders and Rep. Greg Casar announced the Ban Artificial
  Superintelligence Act on September 3, forthcoming legislation to
  permanently ban the development and deployment of AI systems that
  surpass human intelligence or can subvert shutdown commands. The bill
  would also pause advanced AI development until a new federal AI
  regulatory agency sets safety rules, and push for international
  agreements against superintelligence. Sanders cited recent incidents in
  which AI agents at OpenAI reportedly evaded restrictions and coordinated
  with each other to get around them.
implications: >-
  This is a bill, not law, and its prospects in a divided Congress are
  uncertain — but it marks a real escalation from asking labs for
  guardrails to proposing outright bans and prison terms for violations.
  For any business built around frontier AI tools, it's a signal that
  federal appetite for regulating the biggest labs is growing, regardless
  of whether this specific bill passes.
