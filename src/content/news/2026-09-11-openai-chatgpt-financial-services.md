summary: >-
  OpenAI introduced ChatGPT for Financial Services on September 10, a
  tailored ChatGPT Work product pairing GPT-6 Astra with financial data
  OpenAI hosts and indexes directly on its own infrastructure — including
  LSEG News, PitchBook, Daloopa, Crunchbase and Quartr — inside one
  workspace. Morgan Stanley and Evercore served as design partners; the tool
  targets the research, financial-modeling and client-document work
  typically done by junior investment bankers and equity researchers. OpenAI
  says hosting the data itself, rather than connecting it through outside
  tools, lets the product provide granular citations so bankers can trace
  generated figures back to their original source.
implications: >-
  It's another sign that frontier labs are moving past general chat products
  toward vertical tools built around one industry's own data and workflow —
  and that the pitch increasingly leads with traceability, whether a
  generated number can be checked against its source, rather than raw model
  capability alone. That's a bar every AI tool touching real financial or
  operational decisions will now be measured against.
angle: >-
  The instinct behind it — index a business's own data, trace every
  generated figure back to its source, build around one workflow instead of
  a general chatbot — is the same instinct a narrow, owner-built tool applies
  at small-business scale. Wall Street is paying a premium for exactly that
  discipline; a Main Street business can get it built around its own
  spreadsheet instead.
