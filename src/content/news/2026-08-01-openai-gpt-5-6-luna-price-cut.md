summary: >-
  OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 Luna's price 80% and Terra's 20%, effective July 30 —
  Luna now runs $0.20 per million input tokens and $1.20 per million output,
  down from $1 and $6, with its flagship Sol unchanged. OpenAI says Luna
  matches year-old leading models on quality while costing roughly a
  seventeenth as much and running nearly nine times faster, crediting the
  cuts to GPT-5.6 Sol optimizing OpenAI's own GPU software and a 15%+ gain
  from speculative decoding. The same week, OpenAI began giving 100,000
  academic researchers free access to its top model, GPT-5.6 Sol Pro,
  through 2027.
implications: >-
  A task that cost a dollar on last year's leading models now runs about six
  cents on Luna — for any business already budgeting around AI API costs,
  that's a real drop worth re-pricing against, not just a marketing number.
  It also reflects mounting price pressure across the market, including from
  lower-cost Chinese providers, which tends to benefit buyers switching
  models freely more than any single vendor.
