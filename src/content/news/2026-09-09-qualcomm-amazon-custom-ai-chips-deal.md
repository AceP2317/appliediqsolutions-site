summary: >-
  Qualcomm and Amazon announced a multi-generational deal to co-develop
  custom AI inference chips and 1.6-terabit optical interconnects for Amazon
  Web Services' data centers, addressing energy efficiency and internal
  networking bottlenecks. Amazon received a warrant for up to 25 million
  Qualcomm shares, vesting as its chip and networking purchases climb toward
  $60 billion through 2036; 3.75 million shares vested immediately on
  initial commitments. Qualcomm shares rose more than 5% on the news,
  marking the company's biggest push yet into hyperscale data centers,
  building on its earlier custom-chip agreement with ByteDance.
implications: >-
  For any business paying for cloud AI services, this is another sign that
  the chip supply behind those services is being locked up years in advance
  through direct hyperscaler-to-chipmaker deals rather than open-market
  purchases — a dynamic that shapes what compute costs and how reliably
  it's available, long before it shows up on a monthly cloud bill.
