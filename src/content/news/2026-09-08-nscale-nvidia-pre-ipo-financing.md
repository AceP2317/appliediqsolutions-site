summary: >-
  Nscale, a two-year-old British AI infrastructure company planning to go
  public as soon as this month, is reportedly in talks to raise an
  additional $3.5 billion ahead of its IPO — $1.5 billion in convertible
  notes to a group of investors and $2 billion directly from Nvidia, which
  also backed Nscale's $1.1 billion Series B round in March. Nscale recently
  signed a compute deal with Anthropic worth roughly $45 billion, and has
  reportedly told investors it expects about $103 billion in revenue tied to
  that deal — a projection based on signed customer leases, not current
  sales.
implications: >-
  The AI compute build-out keeps drawing outside capital rather than
  slowing, with Nvidia financing both sides of a deal like this one — as
  investor and as the chip supplier the money ultimately flows back to. The
  headline revenue figure is a projection off signed leases, not booked
  sales, a distinction worth remembering whenever a vendor's growth numbers
  get repeated as fact.
