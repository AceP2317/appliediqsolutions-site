summary: >-
  Nvidia and roughly 44 founding companies — including Microsoft, IBM, Cisco,
  Cloudflare, CrowdStrike, Dell, HPE, Databricks, Snowflake, Salesforce, SAP,
  ServiceNow, Palantir, Hugging Face, and the Linux Foundation — launched the
  Open Secure AI Alliance on July 27 to build and share open-source AI
  defense tools. Nvidia's announcement points directly to the OpenAI–Hugging
  Face breach disclosed earlier this month: when closed AI tools couldn't
  distinguish attackers from defenders, Hugging Face turned to an open-weight
  model on its own infrastructure to analyze 17,000+ logged actions and
  contain the intrusion. OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic are not among the
  founding members.
implications: >-
  It's a concrete industry bet that open, inspectable AI tools are necessary
  for cyber defense specifically, not just a philosophical preference —
  because a defender who can't run and adapt the tool on their own
  infrastructure is stuck waiting during exactly the moment speed matters
  most. For any business evaluating AI security tooling, it's worth noting
  which vendors are and aren't part of this coalition, and why.
