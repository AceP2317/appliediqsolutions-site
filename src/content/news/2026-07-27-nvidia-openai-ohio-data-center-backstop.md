summary: >-
  Nvidia is in talks to guarantee roughly $250 billion in financing for a
  10-gigawatt data center OpenAI wants to lease in southern Ohio, developed
  by SoftBank's energy subsidiary, the Wall Street Journal reported July 26
  — a claim Reuters said it could not independently verify. The guarantee
  would cover the site's lease and construction debt, not the chips inside
  it; Nvidia is separately discussing financing up to $350 billion of
  OpenAI's chip purchases. The full project, hardware included, is expected
  to cost more than $500 billion, with its first 800-megawatt phase due in
  2028, and Anthropic, Microsoft and Google have also discussed the site
  with the Commerce Department.
implications: >-
  If it closes, it would be a first step toward OpenAI owning infrastructure
  outright instead of renting compute from Microsoft, Amazon and Oracle — a
  shift worth watching for any business whose AI costs already depend on how
  much compute the big labs can secure. It's also a sign that AI
  infrastructure is increasingly financed through vendor-guaranteed debt
  rather than straight equity, concentrating risk in a handful of backers if
  demand falls short of these bets.
