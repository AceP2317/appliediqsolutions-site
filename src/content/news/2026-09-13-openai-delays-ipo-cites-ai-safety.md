summary: >-
  OpenAI will not pursue an IPO this year, CEO Sam Altman told Fortune,
  calling it "an ill-advised moment to go public" given unresolved AI safety
  and alignment work — pushing a potential trillion-dollar listing once
  discussed for 2026 into 2027. The move follows Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's
  public call to "slow the pace at which we improve the capabilities of AI
  models," which Altman said he agreed with, adding OpenAI needs to "pace the
  frontier." He suggested leading AI companies could be close to a joint
  agreement on safety. Anthropic itself has not slowed its own IPO plans.
implications: >-
  When the two most prominent AI labs both signal they're deliberately
  capping how fast capability advances, it's a rare public acknowledgment
  from inside the industry that the pace of change may be outrunning safety
  work — worth watching for any business timing decisions around what AI
  tools to adopt and when.
