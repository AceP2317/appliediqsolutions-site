summary: >-
  OpenAI expanded Daybreak, its cyber-defense service for approved security
  teams, into two tiers: Blue, offering incident response, malware analysis,
  and patch validation on its frontier models with guardrails adjusted for
  legitimate security work; and Red, which adds a new purpose-built model,
  GPT-5.6-Cyber, for vulnerability research and exploit validation.
  GPT-5.6-Cyber is available only to "trusted customer partners," reported
  to include Accenture, IBM, CrowdStrike, and Cloudflare. OpenAI said threat
  actors will increasingly use AI to run cyberattacks "at unprecedented
  speed and scale, including in fully autonomous ways."
implications: >-
  The same labs building AI systems capable of finding and exploiting
  vulnerabilities are now selling access to defend against them — access
  that, for now, only a handful of large enterprises can buy directly. For
  smaller businesses the takeaway is indirect: the security vendors and
  software they already rely on are the ones who'll need this kind of
  tooling, and it's worth asking whether they have it.
