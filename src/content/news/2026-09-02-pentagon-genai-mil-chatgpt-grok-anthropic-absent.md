summary: >-
  The Department of Defense added custom government versions of OpenAI's
  ChatGPT and xAI's Grok to its GenAI.mil platform on August 31, giving
  roughly 3 million military and civilian personnel access alongside the
  Google Gemini version that launched the platform last December. GenAI.mil
  has onboarded more than 1.7 million users, per the department. ChatGPT
  Mil covers unclassified administrative work; Grok for Government targets
  tasks from market research to supply-chain management. Anthropic's
  Claude remains absent: the Pentagon designated it a "supply-chain risk"
  after it refused to drop guardrails against mass surveillance and
  lethal autonomous weapons use, a dispute it is fighting in court.
implications: >-
  For anyone watching where enterprise AI norms get set, this is a live
  example of a vendor holding a safety line even at the cost of a very
  large government contract — worth noting the next time a platform's terms
  of service change quietly. It also puts three frontier labs, not one,
  inside the federal government's daily workflow, the kind of adoption that
  tends to filter down into contractor requirements and compliance
  expectations over time.
