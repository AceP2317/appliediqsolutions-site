summary: >-
  Anthropic, Google and OpenAI have been discussing forming an industry
  standards body to test advanced AI models before wide release, a plan
  sparked by a July proposal from Google DeepMind's Demis Hassabis for a
  FINRA-style regulator. The talks are separate from a similar plan Treasury
  Secretary Scott Bessent reportedly worked on for the Trump administration,
  which lost momentum after Meta's CEO argued against a national AI
  regulator on a call with the President. No agreement has been reached on
  what the standards would actually require.
implications: >-
  If the effort holds together, it could eventually give businesses and IT
  partners a shared way to compare AI vendors' safety practices, similar to
  how security certifications are used today, instead of relying on each
  vendor's own claims. For now the talks are early, voluntary and
  non-binding, and a parallel government-led version has already stalled
  amid industry pushback.
