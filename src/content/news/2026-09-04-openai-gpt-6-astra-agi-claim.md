summary: >-
  OpenAI began rolling out GPT-6 Astra on September 3, describing it as its
  most intelligent and aligned model yet. The company reports a 98% score on
  FrontierMath Tier 4, 99.9% on ARC-AGI-3, and 100% on ExploitBench, and
  calls Astra state-of-the-art on computer use, browsing, software
  engineering, and cybersecurity. Because Astra meets the "Critical"
  threshold for cybersecurity risk under OpenAI's own safety framework, it is
  rolling out gradually — starting with vetted organizations before reaching
  ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users and the API. OpenAI
  president Greg Brockman told reporters, "Welcome to the AGI era."
implications: >-
  A model OpenAI itself is gating for cybersecurity risk is worth taking at
  face value: the company slow-rolled its own flagship launch rather than
  ship it broadly on day one. For any business already leaning on ChatGPT for
  drafting, research, or code, the near-term effect is a jump in what a paid
  seat can do unsupervised, including running desktop tasks in the
  background — which raises the same question every capability jump does:
  who reviews the output before it reaches a customer or a filing.
