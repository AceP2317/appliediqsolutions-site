summary: >-
  Alibaba's Qwen team released Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter
  mixture-of-experts model that takes text, image, and video input, with a
  hosted API live now and open weights for both Qwen3.8-Max and a smaller
  Qwen3.8-27B checkpoint promised the following week. The hosted API is
  OpenAI- and DashScope-compatible, carries a 1-million-token context
  window, and is priced at $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million
  output tokens. Alibaba published benchmark results putting it in range of
  current OpenAI and Anthropic models on coding and reasoning tests, with
  its biggest gains over its own prior model showing up in multimodal and
  agentic tasks rather than raw reasoning.
implications: >-
  A frontier-scale model shipping with open weights, at prices well below
  many closed alternatives, adds real competitive pressure on the current
  field of paid options. For any business evaluating AI vendors, it's
  another credible, cheaper option to weigh — at least via the smaller
  27B checkpoint, which is the version realistic to run outside a
  specialized data center.
