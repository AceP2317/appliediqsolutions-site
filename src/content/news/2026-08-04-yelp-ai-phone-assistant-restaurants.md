summary: >-
  Yelp expanded its Yelp Host AI phone assistant with new capabilities aimed
  at independent restaurants facing labor shortages and high call volumes.
  The tool answers incoming calls around the clock, takes pickup orders
  conversationally and sends them straight to a restaurant's point-of-sale
  system without staff involvement, and for OpenTable users can book, modify,
  or cancel reservations at any hour. Yelp says Host has now handled over one
  million calls, with call volume growing an average of 38% month over
  month.
implications: >-
  A missed call at a small restaurant is a missed order or a missed
  reservation, and staffing a phone line around business hours has real
  payroll cost. Tools like this aren't replacing front-of-house staff so
  much as covering the calls nobody was answering anyway — after-hours,
  during a rush, or when the phone rings while someone's hands are full. The
  month-over-month growth in call volume suggests demand for this kind of
  coverage was already there and going unanswered.
angle: >-
  This is close to the problem the missed-call / lead-catcher demo we've
  been scoping addresses: a local business loses revenue to calls it can't
  get to, not because it needs a bigger platform, but because nobody covers
  the phone at 9pm on a Tuesday. Yelp's version proves the problem is real;
  it's also a reason a shop wants coverage built around how it runs, not
  bolted onto a directory listing.
