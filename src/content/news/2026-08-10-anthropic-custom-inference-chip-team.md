summary: >-
  Anthropic has started hiring engineers for an in-house team to co-design a
  custom chip built specifically for running Claude's inference workloads,
  rather than training. Custom silicon can cut total cost of ownership by up
  to roughly 65% compared with general-purpose Nvidia GPUs, and Anthropic
  told Business Insider it wants Claude to run faster and more efficiently
  at the scale its customers need as agentic AI use drives far higher token
  volumes than single-prompt chat. Anthropic hasn't named its design
  partner, though it was separately reported last month to be in talks with
  Samsung on manufacturing.
implications: >-
  Every major AI lab now has, or is building, its own chip rather than
  relying solely on Nvidia — a shift driven by cost and power constraints as
  much as performance. If custom silicon meaningfully lowers inference
  costs industry-wide, it's a leading indicator that AI subscription and
  API pricing has room to keep falling, which matters directly to any
  business currently weighing the cost of using AI tools day to day.
