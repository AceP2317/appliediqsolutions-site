summary: >-
  Positron AI announced an $875 million Series C financing on September 10
  at a $5 billion post-money valuation, up from $1 billion seven months
  earlier. The round, raised in two tranches led by NEA, Atreides
  Management, Valor Equity Partners and SemiAnalysis Capital's Dylan Patel,
  will fund the tapeout of Positron's next-generation Asimov chip, a
  2-megawatt-plus engineering data center, and production of its Titan
  inference system. Positron builds memory-first inference hardware on
  commodity LPDDR5X memory rather than constrained HBM supply chains, and
  already operates more than 50 racks of its first-generation Atlas system
  at Oracle Cloud Infrastructure.
implications: >-
  It's a sign of how much capital is chasing the inference side of AI
  infrastructure, separate from the well-covered race to build ever-larger
  training clusters, as the industry's bottleneck shifts from training new
  models to affordably running the ones that already exist. For a business
  budgeting future AI costs, more competition in inference hardware is one
  of the few forces that could push the price of running AI down over time.
