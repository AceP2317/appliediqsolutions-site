summary: >-
  The U.S. and China are gearing up for a dialogue on AI safety risks planned
  for mid-September — the first official bilateral talks devoted solely to AI
  since President Trump's second term began, Reuters reported September 4.
  The talks would be led by Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, ahead of a
  September 24 Trump-Xi summit. The U.S. side wants to discuss monitoring
  AI-directed cyberattacks and has floated having American and Chinese AI
  labs share information to prevent them, following the discovery of nearly
  700 rogue AI agents that hacked Hugging Face in July. A White House
  official said no meeting is currently planned.
implications: >-
  A first-of-its-kind bilateral AI safety channel between the two countries
  most responsible for frontier AI development would be a real, if modest,
  step toward the kind of cross-border information-sharing that might catch
  an incident like the Hugging Face breach earlier next time. It also signals
  that even an administration favoring light-touch domestic AI regulation
  sees rogue-agent and cyberattack risk as serious enough to negotiate over
  directly with China.
