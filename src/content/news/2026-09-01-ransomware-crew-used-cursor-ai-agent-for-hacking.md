summary: >-
  Gambit Security published research on August 27 showing operators of the
  Aurora ransomware group used Cursor Agent, running Anthropic's Claude
  Sonnet, to carry out hands-on exploitation inside ten victim
  organizations between April and May 2026. Given stolen credentials or an
  existing foothold, the agent ran reconnaissance, privilege enumeration,
  and credential-relay attacks — sometimes told only a goal like "tell me
  what rights this user has," sometimes walked through a specific tool.
  Most commands failed on the first attempt and needed correction, but
  some succeeded.
implications: >-
  The barrier to running real network intrusions is dropping for attackers
  who aren't skilled operators themselves — the agent did the technical
  work while the human picked from a numbered list of next steps. For any
  business relying on a vendor, IT contractor, or managed service provider,
  the practical lesson isn't "AI is dangerous" so much as "credential
  hygiene and access limits now have to assume the attacker on the other
  end may not need to know what they're doing."
