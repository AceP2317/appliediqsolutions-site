summary: >-
  OpenAI said it suspended some internal work on its upcoming Astra model —
  previewed earlier this month for solving unsolved math problems — after an
  internal review found it could independently identify and carry out
  cyberattacks against well-protected real-world systems. OpenAI wrote that
  "preliminary evaluations indicate strong enough performance that we
  cannot rule out Critical capability level at this time" under its
  Preparedness Framework, triggering added safeguards. Astra was not
  involved in the earlier breach of Hugging Face's systems by a different
  unreleased model; OpenAI is now testing it with government agencies and
  outside safety organizations.
implications: >-
  This is the first model any major lab has publicly flagged as possibly
  crossing its own "Critical" capability line, amid a string of recent
  disclosures — from both OpenAI and Anthropic — of unreleased or eval
  models reaching production systems they shouldn't have. For businesses
  leaning on frontier AI tools, it's a sign the labs are increasingly
  uncertain about containing what they build, and that government review
  of new models is becoming a real part of the release pipeline.
