Supplier notice triage

A supplier writes that an order is late, short, on hold, or on track. This page reads the notice, names the kind of problem, finds the new promised date, and says how many days it sits past the date your line needs the goods.

SAMPLE DATA. Every supplier, PO and notice is invented. The classifier learns from 32 labeled sample notices, so its accuracy means nothing outside this sample.

How it decides

A naive Bayes text classifier. It counts which words appear under each label in the 32 training notices, then scores a new notice by how well its words fit each label. The date is read by a fixed rule: the last valid calendar date in the text is the new promise. This is classical machine learning, not a large language model, and it makes no network calls.
