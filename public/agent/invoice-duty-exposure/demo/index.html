Invoice duty exposure

SAMPLE DATA. The 36 training descriptions, the invoice lines, the values and every rate below are invented for this demo. They are not HTS rates and not any real supplier's data. Chapter numbers are real HS chapters used as labels only.

Each line's description is assigned to a two-digit HS chapter by a small word-count classifier trained on the sample above. The chapter's sample rate for that origin is then applied to the value. Lines with low confidence are marked for review.
